Fishing Map is a small website where a group of anglers shares fishing spots and logs catches. By asking for an account or using Fishing Map you agree to these terms and to the Privacy page.

Who may join

  • You must be 18 or older to use Fishing Map.
  • You ask for an account and the organizer (Richard) approves each person. He can say no.
  • One person, one account. Keep your password to yourself.

Play fair

  • No cheating and no false catches: don't enter a catch you didn't make, a wrong size or species, or a wrong place to win a contest, race or coins.
  • Be decent. No harassment, hate, spam, or anything illegal. Don't post other people's private details.
  • Follow fishing laws: size and bag limits, closed seasons and no-fishing zones. The app's rules and warnings are a help, not legal advice. You are responsible for checking the current rules.
  • Never trespass to reach a spot, and fish safely. The app's weather, tide and safety information may be wrong or late.

Your content

  • Your spots, catches, photos and messages stay yours.
  • You let Fishing Map store them and show them to the people you chose (Private, Friends or Public), exactly as you set it. That permission ends when you delete the content or your account.
  • Only upload photos and text you have the right to share.

Removing content and people

  • The owner and administrators he chooses can remove content that breaks these terms, hide a public spot, warn a person, or ban a person. Removals can be undone, and bans need a written reason.
  • To fix cheating or mistakes, the owner and chosen administrators can add correcting entries to coins and race results, always with a logged reason.
  • You can delete your own content, or your whole account, in the app.
