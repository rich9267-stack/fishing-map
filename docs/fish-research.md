# Fish seasons & tendencies — Southeast Florida (researched Oct 8, 2026)

Used by the 📅 Seasons tab and 🏆 Best spots today. The data lives in `SEASONS` in index.html.

How it's used:
- **Shore vs boat** months (0 slow, 1 decent, 2 peak). Our spots are all shore spots, so the shore numbers drive the ranking (season = ±0.5).
- **Spawning**: months, where, and whether adults *leave* shore spots (e.g., mangrove snapper Jun–Aug, mutton snapper May–Jul) or *bunch up* within reach of shore (e.g., snook at inlets and beaches May–Sep, sheepshead at jetties Feb–Apr, mullet run Oct–Jan).
- **Water temperature** (salt/brackish spots): below a fish's "cold" number −0.5 ("too cold"), below its comfortable range or above it −0.25. Canal water temps aren't available, so freshwater spots skip this.

Confidence: freshwater (peacock bass, snakehead, Mayan cichlid, clown knifefish), Spanish mackerel, pompano, sheepshead, kingfish, cobia and the snapper spawning times are backed by the sources below. Many inshore shore-timing numbers are best estimates from general Florida fishing knowledge — adjust them as the group's own catches show otherwise.

Key sources:
- FWC species profiles: butterfly peacock, bullseye snakehead, Mayan cichlid, clown knifefish, Spanish mackerel, sheepshead, mullet (myfwc.com)
- Gulf States Marine Fisheries Commission — Gray snapper profile (Atlantic spawning Apr–Nov, peak Jun–Jul, offshore, full moon)
- SEDAR 79 mutton snapper reproduction; GCFI spawning-aggregation review (grouper, mutton)
- SEDAR king mackerel landings in the east Florida mixing zone; SAFMC king mackerel info
- NOAA Fisheries cobia; GCFI cobia movements
- Snook cold tolerance: U. Miami shark research lab (2010 cold kill, stress below 60°F); The Islander (60°F stress, ~55°F lethargy/death)
- Florida Sportsman: pier pompano; peacock bass cold die-offs (2000, 2010)
- The Coastal Star (Palm Beach County Spanish mackerel timing)
- UF/IFAS Charlotte County: sheepshead spawning
