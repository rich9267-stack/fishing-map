What Fishbrain does, how its screens flow, what anglers love and hate about it, and a ranked list of fixes and missing features for our Fishing Map. Everything we'd build stays **$0** and fits our friends-first, no-spot-burning approach.

## The short version
- Fishbrain is the biggest fishing app (10M+ downloads on Android, 4.5–4.7 stars, ~15–20 million members). Its power is **lots of other people's catches on a map**, plus forecasts and depth maps.
- Most of the good stuff is behind **Fishbrain Pro: about $10–13 a month or $75–80 a year**. The paywall is by far the biggest complaint, followed by bugs, slow support, wrong catch locations and a noisy worldwide feed.
- We already match or beat it on: free everything, local conditions (tides, wind, pressure, water temp, solunar), Florida seasons and rules, fish ID, measuring, offline logging, trips, friends/groups, contests, privacy (no exact public pins).
- Biggest gaps for us: **one page per fish species**, **"what's biting near me" on the map**, **fishing license reminders**, **a "report a problem" button**, and **delete-my-account**.

## 2. How its screens flow
1. **Onboarding, 9 steps**: pick your location on a 3D globe, a short quiz (what you fish for, how often).
2. **Paywall right after onboarding**: yearly plan "Save 49%", 3-day free trial, feature carousel. Locked features (depth maps) keep reminding you later.
3. **The map is home**: pins for catches (green = public, red = your private ones), "new" badge on catches from the last 14 days, layers menu top-right (depth, parks, gauges, hot spots), weather icon on the right → weather and BiteTime.
4. **Tap a pin** → species, bait, weather at the time, photo (photos of other people's catches are now Pro-only, per 2026 reviews).
5. **Species** → Top Baits ranked by catch count, BiteTime graph by the hour.
6. **Feed** → catches from everyone (worldwide), comments, likes, groups.
7. **Logbook** → your catches, baits, conditions, stats.

## 3. What anglers say
**Love:** the community ("friendly people"), seeing what others catch and on what bait, finding new local water, logging a catch "with just a snap of a photo", learning which baits and colors work.

**Hate (most mentioned first):**
1. **Paywall and price** — features that used to be free (even looking at other people's catch photos) moved into Pro; surprise $80 yearly charges.
2. **Bugs and crashes** — crashes on launch or on new posts, reinstalling monthly; quality score dropped 15 points in the last 30 days (unitQ, Oct 13, 2026).
3. **Support** — tickets closed without fixes, "robo responses".
4. **Wrong locations** — catches pinned to the wrong water; search drops you "in the Atlantic".
5. **Noisy feed** — catches from other countries, bots and spam; can't filter to recent catches only.
6. **Spot burning** — Outdoor Life calls fishing apps the worst source of burned spots; one small pond had ~30 pins.
7. Spots recommended that turned out to be private or boat-only.

## 6. Don't copy
- **The paywall and ads** — the #1 complaint. Keep everything free.
- **Exact public catch pins** — that's how spots get burned. Keep blurring.
- **A worldwide feed** — keep it friends and local.
- **Tackle marketplace, Bluetooth fish-finder pairing** — not worth it for us (cost, and a web app can't pair reliably).
- **"Bug fixes" release notes** — keep our plain-language What's new.
