# Learn: official / open sources for the 8 knots and 8 rigs (research only, Oct 23, 2026)

Nothing in the app was changed. Question: can the knot cards (data/learn/knots.json) and rig cards (data/learn/rigs.json), now "our own general advice", be backed by an official or openly licensed source?

**How sure these findings are:** page summaries came from a small model, and image licences from Wikimedia's own licence data. I could not read three official PDFs (Illinois DNR knot guide, NY DEC lesson plan, Texas Parks & Wildlife "fishing rigs") because this computer has no PDF reader; their contents below come only from search snippets. Open each by hand before relying on it.

## Terms in plain words
- **State agency pages (Utah, Illinois, NY, Texas):** none showed a reuse licence. Utah's page says "© State of Utah". Treat as **link only, or our own short wording of the facts with a source link** (what we did for the fish cards). Do not copy their sentences or pictures.
- **Wikipedia / Wikibooks text:** Creative Commons **BY-SA 4.0**. We may copy it **if** we credit it and publish our copy under the same licence (share-alike). That would make those cards' text CC BY-SA. Needs a line in the lawyer packet.
- **Wikimedia pictures:** each has its own licence (listed below). Public domain = free. CC BY = free with credit. CC BY-SA = free with credit, share-alike. The cards already have an empty `picture` + `credit` spot.
- **Take Me Fishing (RBFF):** its pictures are "all rights reserved, used by permission" on a US Fish & Wildlife page. No reuse terms found. Do not copy.
- **FWC, NOAA Fisheries, USFWS:** no knot or rig how-to pages found.

## Recommendation (Richard decides)
1. **Keep our wording as "general advice" where no source exists:** Loop knot, Double uni, Pompano rig, Knocker rig. Say so on the card (already does).
2. **Palomar, Improved clinch, Uni:** rewrite the steps in our own short words from the **Utah DWR** page, with "Source: Utah DWR ↗ · checked <date>" like the fish cards. Add the source for the other agency page(s) only after we open them by hand. This is facts-with-link, not copying.
3. **Pictures that cost nothing:** Palomar photos (CC BY), Albright and Nail knot diagrams (public domain), Texas rig diagrams (CC BY). Fill the existing `picture` / `credit` spot. A picture must match the steps beside it, so check each before adding.
4. **Texas rig:** rewrite from Wikipedia's 4 threading steps (CC BY-SA, so credit + share-alike) **or** leave as general advice. If we use any Wikipedia text, add a lawyer-packet line.
5. **Carolina, Drop-shot, Fish-finder, Free-line, Popping cork:** add a small "Official mentions" line from the FWC pages we already use (Snook, Red Drum, Saltwater Fishing Tips); keep the build steps as general advice. Carolina: open the TPWD PDF by hand first.
6. **Not worth it:** copying text or pictures from Take Me Fishing, Bass Pro, Outdoor Life, Wired2Fish and similar (all rights reserved).
