# Competitor audit: Bite ("Bite - Fishing, Evolved")

Checked Oct 26, 2026, from Bite's website (bitefishingapp.com), its App Store page and its privacy policy. Nothing was installed or signed up for. Several other apps also have "Bite" in the name (BiteCast, PrimeBite, Bite Signal, BiteLog, On The Bite). This audit covers the one that calls itself "the social network built around catching fish", which is the closest to us.

## Who they are
- One developer (Jeffrey Graham), "built by anglers, for anglers". First App Store version is from about June 2026; now version 1.4.1 and updating every week or two.
- iPhone app. Android users get a web app for now; a real Android app is "coming later this year".
- 4.8 stars from 95 ratings. Most reviews visible are from their own beta testers. The one complaint: the map is the weakest part.
- Lots of crash / blank-screen fixes in their update notes, so it is still a bit shaky.

## Money
- Free tier is generous: map, catch log, conditions, feed, messages, private pins, state rules, offline maps, leaderboards, badges.
- Bite Pro: $9.99/month or $59.99/year in the App Store (their website says $14.99 / $69.99, so the two don't match).
- Also sells: tournament pass $7.99, tournament entry $10, "boosts" $1.99–$4.99, a shop that sells from videos (affiliate links), charter-captain listings for Pro, a $28.98 T-shirt, and donations.
- Their privacy label says a device ID is used **to track you for third-party advertising**, and the app has ads (Pro removes them).

## What this means
1. **We are not behind.** On maps, rules, protected waters, learning and fun (tanks, games) we have more. Their own reviewers say their map is weak, and the map is our strongest part.
2. **The real difference is the type of app.** Bite is a public social network paid for by ads, a shop and boosts. We are a private, friends-first app with no ads. Keep it that way; it's easy to explain: "your spots stay with your crew."
3. **Their 0.1-mile blur is weak.** 500 feet often gives away a dock or a hole. Worth saying plainly in our own marketing later (after the lawyer review).
4. **They move fast and talk to users** (Discord, weekly updates). Our version of that is the What's new box; fine for now.

## Ideas worth borrowing (small, $0)
- **One simple number for "how good is today"** (like their 0–10 score) on Home, built from what Home already shows.
- **An evening "tomorrow looks good" phone alert** for your favourite spot.
- **Weekly challenges** (e.g. "catch 3 different fish this week") with badges, no money.
- **Fill in time and place from the photo itself** when you log a catch from an old photo.
- **A month calendar** of good days (tide + moon), next to Plan a trip.

## Not worth copying
- AI chat guide (costs money for every question; our budget is $0).
- Ads, boosts, shop, paid tournament entries (go against our no-money / invite-only plan and need the lawyer).
- Kelp paddy tracker (California offshore only).
