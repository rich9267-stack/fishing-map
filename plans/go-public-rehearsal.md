# Stranger rehearsal — run on your phone before Oct 27 (about 25 minutes)

Goal: walk the app the way a brand-new person would, and write down anything that confuses you, looks wrong, or fails.
Nothing here needs code. If a line doesn't show what it says, note the line number and what you saw (a screenshot helps).

**Marks:** 📱 = one phone, one account. 📱📱 = needs two phones, or one phone plus a second browser/profile, and two accounts.

## Before you start (3 min)
- **Rehearsal email (a "stranger"):** use a plus address of your Gmail, e.g. `richard9267+rehearsal1@gmail.com`. Mail arrives in the same inbox, and the app treats it as a new person. If you rehearse again later, use `+rehearsal2`.
- **Rehearsal Google account (optional):** a Google account that is not a member. If you don't have one, skip lines 9–10 and write "skipped".
- **Your own account** (the owner, rich9267@gmail.com) stays signed in on your normal phone or browser to approve. The stranger runs in a private/incognito tab, or a second phone.
- Hard-refresh first: open the site, pull down to refresh, and check What's New says v140 or newer.
