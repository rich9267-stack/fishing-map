# Prompt: full team meeting — "Ready to go public" (paste into a NEW session)

```
I'm Richard (Pompano Beach, no programming experience). Call a full team meeting of all our roles
(Engineer, Tester, IT, Legal, Designer/UX, Product, Support/Community — use whichever roles we have; skip none).
Read first: CLAUDE.md, docs/security.md, plans/step9-open-questions.md, plans/database-change-rule.md,
docs/roadmap.md and docs/parked_ideas.md.

Facts:
- Fishing Map v140 is live and invite-only. Budget is $0 right now.
- I start a new job on Oct 27. I will not be paid until about 3-4 weeks later (late Nov). After that I can pay
  for a lawyer and an accountant review, then open the app to the public.
- Legal review of terms.html and privacy.html is postponed until I'm paid. Nothing is lawyer-approved.
- Security checklist (docs/security.md) is done. Members are 18+. No privacy promise may be broken.

Goal of the meeting: decide what we can do for FREE between now and the day money comes in, so that on that day
we can have the review done and go public quickly with no surprises.

Each role gives, in plain words and max 5 bullets:
1. What is still missing or risky from your side for going public.
2. What you can do for free before late Nov (be specific, small steps I can check on my phone).
3. What must wait for money or the lawyer/accountant, and the rough cost if known.

Then the team agrees on:
- A ranked list of the next 5-8 free tasks, each with who does it, size (small/medium), and how I check it on my phone.
- A "go-public day" checklist: what the lawyer/accountant must review (terms, privacy, age line, prizes/contests rules,
  coins/gems, any money questions), what I do in Supabase/GitHub/Google, what we test, how we invite the first strangers
  (small waves), and how we roll back.
- Things we should NOT build yet.
- Because I'm busy at the new job: a low-effort weekly routine (what I check, what the Engineer does alone, what waits for me).

Rules: plain language, no jargon, short. Do not build anything and do not change files or the database in the meeting.
Write the result to plans/go-public-plan.md and end with a ready-to-paste prompt for the first task. I say "Commit" before anything is committed.
```
