🎣 Fishing Map — Privacy

Last updated October 8, 2026

Fishing Map is a small, invite-only website that a group of friends uses to share fishing spots and log catches. It is not a business and has no ads.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and the name you choose. If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name and email address from Google.
  • What you add: spots, catches, notes, and photos you choose to upload.
  • Location: only when you tap a button that uses it (such as "Where I am", "Near me", or the quick-catch camera), to place a spot or match a catch to a spot.

How it's used and shared

  • Your spots, catches and photos are visible to the other invited members of the group. Spots you mark Private (and their catches and photos) are visible only to you.
  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone else, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • Photos are shrunk on your phone before upload; the uploaded copy doesn't keep the hidden details (like GPS) that phones store inside photo files.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database, sign-in and photo storage). The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Weather, tide, and map information is fetched from public sources (NOAA, the National Weather Service, USGS, Open-Meteo, and OpenStreetMap); these services may see your device's internet address when your browser loads their data.

Deleting your information

You can delete your own catches and photos in the app. To have your account and everything you've added removed, contact the site's organizer, Richard, and it will be deleted.
