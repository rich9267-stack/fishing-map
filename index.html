📲 How to add this app to your Home Screen
📷 Photos taken in the app

Android saves a copy by itself. iPhone doesn't let websites save to Photos silently, so you get a one-tap “Save to Photos” button after each shot. The app also keeps the last 14 days of shots here, just in case.

🗺 Offline maps

Save the part of the map you are looking at so it still works with no signal. Zoom the map to your fishing area, then tap Save map for offline under Layers.

Your data

Download your own catches and spots as spreadsheet files (CSV) — they open in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

🗑 Delete my account

Removes your spots, catches, photos, messages, friends and profile, and your sign-in. It can't be undone.

First, save a copy? Download your catches and spots as spreadsheet files before they're gone.

🎨 Colors

Pick the colors you like. Only the colors change; everything stays in the same place. Free, and saved to your account.
