📲 How to add this app to your Home Screen
Looks at moving tide, dawn/dusk light, falling pressure, light wind and your own catches. Includes nearby friends' and public spots you can see. It stays quiet on ordinary days. Location: your saved spots' middle until you set it.
Storm warnings and 🛟 check-in alerts always get through, even in quiet hours.
Alerts that arrive in quiet hours don't buzz your phone. Test alerts always do.

      Ranks your spots by how well the next 12 hours match the conditions when fish were caught there (tide, pressure, time, solunar, wind, water temperature) — nudged up when it’s the season for what’s caught there and when people have photographed those fish nearby lately.
